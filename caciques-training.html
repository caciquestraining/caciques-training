Meet Your Coach
Built by a Player.Built for Players.

Coach Alejandro grew up in Punto Fijo, Venezuela, where baseball wasn't just a sport — it was a way of life. He started playing at just 3 years old, and by age 10 was training every single day with one goal in mind: professional baseball.

At 12, he left home to join a baseball academy in another state, fully committed to his dream. That dedication paid off — at just 16 years old, he played a professional season with Los Tiburones de La Guaira in Venezuela's professional league. Throughout his career he received multiple offers from professional teams, a testament to the talent he built through relentless work.

After retiring at 18, Alejandro channeled everything into coaching — building his career from Punto Fijo to Valencia to Aruba, and working with professional players including MLB athletes. Today he brings that world-class experience to every session at Caciques Training LLC.
